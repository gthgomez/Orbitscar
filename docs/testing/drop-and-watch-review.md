# Drop-and-Watch Review

The baseline strategy is: deploy all legal units as early as capacity permits, use the obvious west approach, and fire the commander ability as soon as it is available. The deliberate alternatives are the north/south flank and the delayed split from the tactical fixtures.

Headless evidence shows non-convergence: frontal is attacker/full at 1228 ticks with 3 casualties, flank is attacker/partial at 993 ticks with 2 casualties, and delayed is attacker/partial at 852 ticks with 1 casualty. The alternatives also destroy different structure sets.

This is a product-risk result, not a success claim. The current data proves that the simulation exposes meaningful levers; it does not prove that a human understands those levers or considers them fun. The next gate is blind testing using [first-combat-test-protocol.md](../ux/first-combat-test-protocol.md).
