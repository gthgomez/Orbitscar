# Edgeworld Screen Inventory

**Status:** RESEARCH BASELINE — principles only
**Access date:** 2026-08-30

This is an evidence-derived interaction inventory, not a layout to reproduce. Screens are reconstructed from reviews, guides, videos/indexes, and community documentation; confidence is shown per surface.

| Surface | Purpose | Evidence | Effective principle | Modern transformation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Colony HUD | Read resources, status, and available actions | Medium | Keep the colony visible while exposing only relevant context | World-first canvas with a compact resource rail and one contextual action tray |

## Principles to preserve

1. The board remains the primary object; menus are instruments.
2. Every important decision shows its consequence before commitment.
3. Reports are first-class play, not an error log.
4. Defense and offense expose the same readable vocabulary.
5. No critical action depends on hover, tiny icons, or unexplained abbreviations.

## Principles to reject

- Dense persistent icon strips.
- Modal chains that hide the colony/map.
- Exact screenshot or panel composition from archived material.
- Long timers as the primary retention mechanic.
- Unreadable combat where placement cannot be linked to the result.
