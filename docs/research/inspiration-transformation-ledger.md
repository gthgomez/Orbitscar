## Mandatory transformation rules

- No original Edgeworld names, factions, locations, logos, unit silhouettes, buildings, icons, map geometry, text, audio, or exact progression values.
- No use of archived SWF, screenshots, textures, ripped models, or copied fan assets as production inputs.
- Design reviews must describe a feature using our own fiction and constraints first.
- A reference may inform a problem statement, but not a tracing target.
- If a reviewer can identify a one-to-one counterpart from a screenshot, redesign the expression.

## Originality test

Every feature passes only when all three answers are yes:

1. **Recognition:** a former player can recognize the broad lineage.
2. **Confusion:** a reasonable observer cannot mistake the work for Edgeworld.
3. **Independent creation:** the feature is explainable from our own world and rules without naming the reference implementation.
