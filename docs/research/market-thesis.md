# Market Thesis

**Status:** CONDITIONAL — do not treat as demand proof
**Access date:** 2026-08-30

## Thesis under test

There is room for a small, original cross-platform strategy game if it owns a narrow experience: fast, readable, asynchronous base attacks where placement and counter-selection matter, followed by a geographic alliance layer that produces stories without demanding daily officer labor.

## Why a player might choose it

- Clash of Clans offers polished attack clarity but a different fantasy and mature content scale; our opportunity is a more inspectable sci-fi fortification and a slower, more analytical breach.
- Last War and Whiteout Survival offer enormous live-ops ecosystems; our opportunity is restraint, transparent combat, and less hero/power inflation.
- Star Trek Fleet Command offers licensed identity and galaxy breadth; our opportunity is original fiction and stronger colony-layout agency.
- Infinite Lagrange offers fleet-scale space strategy; our opportunity is a single colony as a legible battlefield.
- An Edgeworld revival offers nostalgia and exact familiarity; our opportunity is a clean-room game with modern input, replays, accessibility, and a survivable economy.

## Strongest skeptical answer

The project may have no viable acquisition channel. Former Edgeworld players are a small and nostalgia-biased audience; new strategy players already have polished incumbents; a fair cosmetics-first economy may monetize too slowly; territory requires a critical mass; and a browser/mobile indie cannot match live content. “Edgeworld lineage” is not a moat and may be an IP-risk signal.

## Defensible niche, if any

The only defensible niche currently supported is a product-quality promise:

> Every attack is a short strategic experiment: scout, commit, place, intervene, learn.

Territory is a multiplier for social stories, not the acquisition hook. The niche is defensible only if blind tests show repeat desire and reports change subsequent behavior.

## Go/no-go gates

**GO to prototype:** 8–12 blind testers can complete two attacks, explain at least one counter, and ask for another without being prompted.

**CONDITIONAL GO to alpha:** 30-day cohort retains through at least four meaningful attacks, alliance participation is optional, and cosmetics/convenience do not decide competitive outcomes.

**NO-GO or pivot:** testers call it a generic base builder, attacks are solved by power, or the alliance map is the only reason to return.

## Market evidence

Sensor Tower reports mobile games returning to growth in 2024 and identifies strategy as a major spend category; its 2024 outlook says 4X strategy remained the largest strategy subgenre. This validates category spending, not this product’s audience. Cross-platform expectations and Android form-factor requirements increase the engineering surface.
