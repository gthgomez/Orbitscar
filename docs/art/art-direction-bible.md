# Project Orbitscar — Art Direction Bible

**Status:** ART DECISION V0.1

Scores are internal design judgments, not research facts.

## Choice

Choose a stylized orbital-industrial diorama: high-angle camera, modular plinths, exaggerated silhouettes, hard surface shapes softened by weathering, restrained emissive accents, and asymmetric salvage engineering. Buildings should look repaired, rerouted, and purpose-built rather than like a military catalog. The colony is a working place with habitation, conduits, cranes, and sensor arrays.

## Art grammar

- Silhouette first; details only where they improve targeting or ownership.
- Three material families: matte ceramic, worn alloy, translucent energy membrane.
- Faction identity through geometry and material process, never color alone.
- Units use one strong body shape plus a readable tool/weapon; no tiny visual noise.
- Terrain uses broad value blocks, not realistic texture density.
- VFX use shape language: arcs for control, cones for area, rings for impact, threads for repair.
- UI uses dark translucent panels with technical notches and paper-like report cards; no copied ornamental frames.

## Originality gate

The direction is acceptable only if a former player recognizes the broad colony-defense lineage but cannot name a one-to-one counterpart for a building, unit, UI frame, map tile, or portrait.
