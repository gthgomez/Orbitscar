# Orbitscar V1 Campaign Mission

Build the current Orbitscar checkout into a cohesive, replayable deterministic closed-alpha strategy game. Preserve its original setting, clean-room provenance, TypeScript/pnpm monorepo, Phaser client, data-driven content, and authoritative simulation separated from rendering.

The player loop is persistent colony development and bounded production, progression and force composition, scouting and approach choice, incremental combat decisions, commander intervention, retreat or victory, explanatory report/replay, survivor and salvage settlement, defense of the same colony, and progression through PvE, asynchronous rival bases, and connected relay territory.

Completion requires meaningful player decisions and observable consequences across economy, base geometry, research, combat, progression, and territory; no dominant universal strategy; deterministic saves, commands, results and reports; a usable first session; substantially improved original presentation; browser certification against a production build; and adversarial product review. Human fun/legibility playtests must be reported as unverified unless humans actually perform them.

Edgeworld is high-level strategic inspiration only. Do not copy its expressive content, names, assets, maps, text, factions, or layout. Keep combat authority in `packages/simulation`, content declarative in `packages/content`, and rendering/client state in `apps/web`. Prefer small integrated systems and local substitutes for optional external services.
