# Orbitscar V1 Campaign Status

- **Current milestone:** 5/10 — balance and defensive raid progression; reactive choices are evaluated, live stakes are visible, and the player's persistent colony now faces escalating raid forces.
- **Completed:** bounded production/economy and recovery; same-colony NPC raids with damage, repairs, reports, replay, and three escalating force bands; deterministic incremental attack commands, reinforcements, commander choices, and retreat; persistent doctrines, Command Tiers, unlocks, objectives, and scout-gated intelligence; obstacle-aware approaches; 11 authored PvE relays; local authoritative profile/rival/PvE APIs with versioned snapshots, idempotent settlement, reports/replays; connected sector ownership with adjacency gates, solo progression, and repeat-reward limits; ruleset 0.5.0 unit weaknesses on direct hits and splash; ruleset 0.6.0 factual scouting/report language; ruleset 0.7.0 balanced approach layouts and validated structure footprints; ruleset 0.8.0 deterministic defense target locks, visible acquisition/reload windows, and report attribution; ruleset 0.9.0 air-only Snare Lattice splash counter and reproducible reactive reinforcement evaluation; ruleset 0.10.0 retreat salvage suppression and event-derived live battle ledger; original SVG art, procedural silhouettes, defense range overlays, synthesized UI/battle cues with saved mute control, and schema 10 persisted sortie and defensive-engagement counts.
- **Branch / base:** `fix/balance-scenario-tests`, latest implementation commit `5e4ed4a feat: escalate persistent colony raids`.
- **Evidence:** paired ruleset 0.8.0 vs 0.9.0 full evaluation used 4,928 runs per ruleset (2,464 plans × two seeds), with zero invariant violations and 4,400 unique outcomes per corpus. The final air-filtered Snare counter reduced air-harass from 82.4% to 73.0% overall and raised its casualties by 0.63 per battle; its three no-snare encounters remain unchanged at 90.1%. All other compositions' win rates/casualties and every no-snare outcome are identical to 0.8.0. In the ruleset 0.10.0 reactive timing corpus (6,160 runs, seed base 30,000), counter-read won 76.1% versus immediate mass 74.5%, while taking 0.30 more casualties and gaining 0.87 more alloy per battle; reactive improved the winner in 114 and worsened it in 95 of 1,232 paired cases. A follow-up two-policy experiment used 7,392 paired runs on each of seeds 40,000 and 100,000; the conditional same-lane-on-defense-damage policy did not improve aggregate win rate and was not retained, though it slightly increased full-breach and alloy outcomes. Fixed schedules remain weak (probe 66.0%, half-half 56.3%, thirds 45.4%). The approach rates from ruleset 0.9.0 span 57.4–64.4%. The shared validator rejects battle windows above 2,400 ticks; all production constructors already use this 80-second cap. Its explicit 2,400/2,401 boundary regression passes. Under ruleset 0.10.0, extractor-then-retreat produces no salvage; the live ledger tests event-derived casualties, disabled defenses, salvage potential, and withdrawal. Schema 10 escalates each of three NPC raid archetypes through force capacities 6/8/10 after every three defensive engagements; migration, settlement, all nine force bands, capacity limits, and the home-colony escalation readout are covered. The latest `pnpm check` passed all root/web/server typechecks, content validation, all 104 tests across 14 files, and production build. The production bundle is 1,341.62 KB minified (372.62 KB gzip). No browser automation has run.
- **Known risks/gaps:** air-harass still leads the full campaign aggregate, though its counter matchup is now more consequential. Fixed staged schedules remain weak; counter-read has a small average win advantage over immediate mass but trades additional casualties for salvage. Hollow Meridian remains the harshest authored relay; Snare matchup severity and raid escalation need human play validation. Updating the combat ruleset discards any unfinished saved battle from a previous ruleset; prior reports remain available. Vite's production bundle has previously warned that the main client chunk is about 1.34 MB minified (373 KB gzip). Authority client flow is browser-unvalidated; production static hosting needs a reverse proxy to the loopback API. Server persistence is local single-process file storage without network authentication. Human blind-playtest evidence remains unverified.
