# Project Orbitscar — Onboarding

**Status:** DESIGN V0.1

## First 15 minutes

| Time | Beat | System revealed | Success signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Stabilize the landing plinth | camera, tap/drag, place one building | player sees colony at all times |
| 3–5 min | Restore a foundry | short queue, three resources | player makes a cost choice |
| 5–8 min | Repel a sensor probe | one defense, target tags | defense placement has a visible consequence |
| 8–12 min | Breach an abandoned relay | scout, capacity, two drops, one ability | player completes first attack |
| 12–15 min | Read the trace | report, replay, sector reveal | player chooses an upgrade or second plan |

## Reveal rules

- One new concept per objective; no alliance invitation during the first battle.
- Use authored NPC defenses to teach one counter at a time.
- Do not show store, season pass, alliance chat, or deep research until the player has seen a report.
- Every objective can be completed without spending premium currency or waiting for a long timer.
- At the end of onboarding, the player may continue solo, join a recommended alliance, or create a private test crew.

## Recovery and accessibility

- If a player fails, the game offers a replay and a “try a different plan” button, not a power sale.
- Tutorial battles can be replayed with no account cost.
- Text is concise, with explicit tags and icon-plus-label pairing.
- Touch targets are at least 48dp; critical actions require a confirm step.
