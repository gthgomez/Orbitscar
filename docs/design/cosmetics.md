# Project Orbitscar — Cosmetics and Identity

**Status:** DESIGN V0.1 — no purchases implemented

## Cosmetic categories

- colony plinth materials and environmental themes;
- building paint, light, antenna, and maintenance variants;
- unit chassis colors and non-silhouette attachments;
- commander protective wear and toolkits;
- deployment, repair, projectile, and destruction VFX;
- alliance banners, node markers, and map signal styles;
- profile frames, titles, monuments, holograms, and seasonal trophies.

## Social visibility

Cosmetics appear in colony visits, scouting snapshots, attack/replay viewers, alliance map markers, battle reports, profile cards, and alliance showcase boards. Combat readability wins: cosmetic effects are intensity-capped and never obscure target tags or hit feedback.

## Integrity rules

- No cosmetic changes hitboxes, range, target priority, speed, capacity, loot, or repair time.
- Competitive modes use a high-contrast fallback silhouette for all units.
- Seasonal trophies are earned, not purchased; paid versions may recolor but not duplicate the exact prestige mark.
- No limited-time cosmetic may be the only way to read a mechanic.
