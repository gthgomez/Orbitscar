# Monetization Options — Adversarial Comparison

**Status:** RECOMMENDATION FOR VALIDATION ONLY — no purchases implemented
**Access date:** 2026-08-30

| Model | Revenue potential | Trust | Competitive integrity | Retention risk | Complexity | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A. Cosmetics-first | Low-to-medium early, high if identity spreads | High | High | Low power churn; cosmetic fatigue | Medium | Default test |
| B. Cosmetics + season pass | Medium and forecastable | Medium-high if rewards are cosmetic | High | Season pressure can exclude casuals | Medium | Candidate after retention proof |
| C. Cosmetics + moderate convenience | Medium | Medium | Medium if convenience touches queues | Medium | High | Only after economy telemetry |
| D. Traditional 4X power/timers | High short-term | Low | Low | High churn, whale dominance | High | Reject for this product |

## Proposed rules

- Free players receive the full combat roster and all counter knowledge.
- Paid items are colony themes, identity packs, profile/social surfaces, and optional queue convenience that never changes daily combat capacity.
- No premium currency can buy a unit, research node, territory capture, scout truth, or battle result.
- A season pass may contain cosmetics, report frames, emotes, and archive fiction; no combat stats.
- No loot boxes in V1. If random cosmetics are ever tested, odds and item pools are disclosed and the purchase is optional.

## Sustainability challenge

Cosmetics-first only works if the game creates repeated social visibility and a sufficiently distinct art language. A small team cannot assume whales will subsidize the product. Validate with a free prototype, then run a willingness-to-pay test on mock cosmetics without charging. If fewer than 10% of engaged testers can name a cosmetic they want to show others, pivot identity surfaces before implementing commerce.

## Evidence

The market supports strategy spending, but current competitors often combine heroes, live events, and convenience monetization. This table is a deliberate product choice, not a claim that a fair model is proven sustainable.
